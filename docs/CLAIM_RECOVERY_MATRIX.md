# Incrimination Nation — Recovery and Accountability Matrix

Prioritize individual, provable remedies rather than a single unsupported multi-party scheme. This matrix is a research worklist, not a finding of misconduct or a valuation of damages.

| Track | Potential remedy | Essential evidence before asserting a claim | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Consumer reporting (FCRA) | Correction, statutory remedies in qualifying circumstances, actual damages where established | Unredacted report stored privately, exact inaccurate field, whether and to whom report was furnished, consumer dispute and response, demonstrable loss | Request complete disclosure and use a dated dispute / response ledger; research which FCRA provisions apply to the specific reporting actor |
| Mohave court / MVD notices | Procedural relief, record correction, administrative review, or civil remedy if every element and immunity limit is met | Certified minute entries, predicate suspension identifiers, issuance and receipt of notices, hearing history, actual injury | Request certified records; maintain a dated table separating court actions, MVD actions, and FARE/TTEAP actions |
| Ventura 2007 | Records correction and possible time-sensitive remedies if supported | Arrest, disposition, warrant and agency documents; precise actor and conduct; accrual/tolling review | Obtain certified disposition and relevant public-record responses; assess limitations before any damages theory |
| Aztec / UCC | Defensive clarification, record correction, or civil claim if independently supported | Actual UCC filings, correspondence, legal relationship, economic transactions, losses | Build a source-to-claim timeline; do not infer collusion from appearance in entity databases |
| Services / operating revenue | Evidence organization, public-record retrieval, searchable archives, and data-quality audits | Customer contract, clear scope, confidentiality, delivery record, transparent pricing | Offer records organization and factual audit services; avoid practicing law or promising settlement outcomes |

## Claim-level proof template

For each proposed remedy, keep a private matrix with: alleged act; actor; date; primary document and its SHA-256; specific legal duty; proof of breach; causation; losses backed by records; counterevidence; deadlines; and whether notice or a complaint was actually delivered. A GitHub document is not proof of service.

## 30-day operating sequence

Week 1: Preserve originals, map existing identifier exposure, obtain the newest certified court/MVD docket and records. Week 2: Reconcile conflicts and build evidence-ledger entries. Week 3: Prepare factual, recipient-specific dispute/records-request drafts and review service channels. Week 4: Follow up on responses and compile documented damages or a records-services portfolio. Do not demand payment without a legally supportable basis.
